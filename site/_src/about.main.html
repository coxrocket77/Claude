About us

In business since 1991 because we finish what we start.

Most contractors in Dallas don't make it five years. We've made it 35. Not because we're the cheapest or the loudest, but because people who hire us once tend to hire us again.

Our story

We started in Addison in 1991 with a simple idea: a contractor should be easy to reach, honest about cost, and still around when you need them next.

Back then, the Metroplex was growing fast and so was the number of contractors who'd take a deposit and disappear. We watched clients get burned and decided to build the company we'd want to hire ourselves.

That meant picking up the phone. It meant writing bids people could actually read. It meant one person accountable for the whole job instead of a chain of subcontractors pointing fingers.

Three decades later, Dallas–Fort Worth looks completely different. The way we work doesn't. We've built out offices and storefronts, turned apartment units by the thousands of square feet, and rehabbed homes for families and investors who needed it done right the first time.

If you're reading this, you probably have a project that matters to you and a worry about who to trust with it. That worry is exactly why we exist.
