Questions

Straight answers before you call.

Don't see yours? Call (214) 559-5799 and talk to someone who can actually answer it.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Addison and work across Dallas–Fort Worth, including Dallas, Plano, Richardson, Carrollton, Farmers Branch, Irving, Frisco, and surrounding cities. If you're outside the Metroplex, call and ask. Larger commercial and multifamily projects often make sense for us to travel.

Is the walkthrough really free?

Yes. We meet you on site, look at the scope, and talk through budget and timing. No cost and no obligation. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you.

How do you price a project?

You get a fixed, itemized bid after the walkthrough. Every line shows what it covers. The number only changes if you ask for a change, and we price every change in writing before doing the work.

Are you insured, and who does the licensed trade work?

We carry general liability and workers' compensation coverage, and licensed electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians perform that work as Texas requires. Ask for certificates at your walkthrough and we'll provide them.

Can you work in an occupied building?

That's a big part of what we do. Apartment communities, open businesses, and lived-in homes all get phased schedules, dust control, and daily cleanup so residents, customers, and families can keep going.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope, permits, and materials, so we give you a written schedule with your bid rather than a guess on the phone. Unit turns often run days; full commercial build-outs run weeks to months.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We pull the permits, schedule inspections, and meet the inspector on site. You don't have to deal with the city.
