The problem

Vendors charge tax by default. Exemptions live on the line.

Texas exempts a long list of what manufacturers buy, but the exemption depends on how you use each item, and your vendor has no idea. So they charge 8.25% and move on. Your AP team pays the invoice. Nobody goes back.

8.25%

Charged on things you use to make product

Machining centers, bar stock, carbide inserts, shielding gas, shipping cartons. If it goes into the product or gets consumed making it, the tax on it is often refundable.

2×

Sometimes paid twice

When a vendor charges sales tax and your team also self-accrues use tax on the same invoice, you pay the state twice. In the sample, an argon refill did exactly that.

4 yrs

And the window keeps closing

Texas allows refund claims for four years. Every month, another month of invoices ages out. The sample shop lost a $7,920 refund on a CNC lathe to the clock.

How it works

Software does the reading. A licensed preparer signs.

Reverse audits used to mean weeks of a consultant paging through invoices. Recoup reviews the whole ledger in one pass and hands a person only the lines that need judgment.

  1. Send your AP export

    A CSV from your accounting system with vendor, invoice, description, amount, tax and GL account. Column names don't need to match ours.

  2. Two reviewers, independently

    One classifies each purchase. A second, skeptical reviewer classifies it again without seeing the first answer. If they disagree, a person decides.

  3. Rules compute every dollar

    AI never does tax math. Deterministic code applies Texas rules, statute citations included, to each classified line.

  4. You get a signed-off packet

    Vendor refund letters, a state claim schedule, and a short list of documents we need from you. A licensed preparer approves before anything leaves.

Why Recoup is different

Questions

What controllers ask first.

Will filing a refund claim trigger an audit?

It can open the period to a closer look. That's why Recoup reports your use tax exposure next to the refund and blocks the packet when exposure runs above 25% of the refund. You decide how to handle it with full information.

Who files the claim?

A licensed tax professional reviews every line, approves the packet and files it. Recoup drafts. It never files, submits or emails anything on its own.

What do you need from us?

One CSV export of your accounts payable detail with vendor, invoice number, date, description, amount, sales tax, use tax and GL account. After the review, you get a short list of anything else that would unlock more refunds, like a utility study or an exemption certificate.

How far back can we go?

Texas allows four years. Recoup flags anything older as expired so nobody spends time on it, and it shows you what aged out so you can see the cost of waiting.

How is AI involved, and how do you keep it honest?

AI reads descriptions and sorts each purchase into a fixed list of categories. It never computes tax. Two reviewers classify each line independently, and any disagreement goes to a person. Plain code applies Texas rules to compute every dollar.

When a line is unclear, Recoup marks it unknown instead of guessing.

Which states do you cover?

Texas today. If you buy into other states, tell us which ones when you send your export.
